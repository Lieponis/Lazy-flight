HOW TO PLAY

Jobs

Tap JOBS at any dock. Pick a delivery: cargo, passengers or a sightseeing tour. Watch the trip (or don't), and get paid when you arrive.

Fuel

Every light year costs fuel. REFUEL at a dock. Stuck with no fuel and no money? There is a free EMERGENCY FUEL button. Nobody gets stranded.

Free travel and discovery

Open MAP, tap any planet and choose FLY HERE. The first visit to a new place pays a small discovery bonus and moves you up the Explorer ranks, from Lost in Space to Mr Galaxy-wide.

Ranks, ships and outposts

Deliveries earn ranks (from Box Pusher to Freight Baron). The SHIPYARD sells three ships, upgrades and paint jobs. You can even buy outposts for cheaper fuel and a little income.

Sound

The speaker at the top mutes everything. MENU → SETTINGS has separate sliders for music and sound effects. On iPhone the silent switch mutes the game, and your own music keeps playing.

INSTALL THIS GAME

Install it like an app. There's no app store and nothing to pay. After the first visit it works without internet.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open the game in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the game in Chrome.
  2. Tap INSTALL in the game, or the ⋮ menu → Install app.

Computer (Chrome or Edge)

Click the small install icon at the right end of the address bar.

Updates

New versions download by themselves. When one is ready, the game shows NEW VERSION READY while you're docked. Tap REFRESH, or just open it again later.

MOVE YOUR SAVE

Your progress lives on your device. The installed app on iPhone keeps its own save, separate from Safari. To move a game to another phone, computer or the installed app:

  1. Open MENU → SAVE FILE → EXPORT. Download the file, or copy the text.
  2. On the other device: MENU → SAVE FILE → IMPORT. Choose the file or paste the text.
  3. Check what's in it (credits, ships, rank), then confirm. Changed your mind? UNDO LAST IMPORT.

Tip: export a save now and then. It's your insurance.
