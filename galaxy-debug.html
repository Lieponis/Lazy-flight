A test page for the big galaxy idea: a flat spiral disc, 100,000 light years across, about 18 billion systems, and nothing is stored: every system is worked out from the seed when it is asked for. Nothing here is part of the game. Back to the game.

2. The whole galaxy from above

Same colours. The disc is 100,000 ly wide but only 500 to 2,000 ly thick, so the height is stretched 12 times here.
