# Out of scope

## Permanently out (never, at any stage)

- Free flight
- Combat

## Out for version 1

- Planet landing
- Multiplayer
- Voxel terrain
- Accounts or servers
- Monetization
- More than 3 ships and 3 ranks
- Full-galaxy map

If someone suggests one of these, label it "suggestion", add it to the report, and do not build it.
