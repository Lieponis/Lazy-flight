# Agent 4 report, stage F1 (4f): free travel and the Explorer rank (game rules and save)

Branch `free-travel`, merged to `main` after a green branch check on Actions (types, build and every test). Andy checks on the live `/game-debug.html`.

## 1. What was built
- **Free travel (no job).** `game.canFreeTravel(id)` gives the numbers (fuel needed, fuel left, time, what exploring would pay) or the reason in plain text; `game.freeTravel(id)` starts the trip. The target is a dock or a planet without a dock, in the same star system or another. Fuel uses the same formula as jobs (engine upgrades count), time is the same as a job of that length (20 s inside a system, 1 min per ly plus 20 s, never over 4 h). No cargo, no pay, no job rank progress, no job-pay change.
- **Where you are.** New state fields (all with defaults, old saves stay docked): `spacePlanetId` (null = docked; else the dock-less planet you float at; `currentDockId` stays your last dock), `freeTrip` (the trip on its way; it reuses `travelStartMs/EndMs`), `explored`. Docked at a free-travel target dock works like any docking: board, refuel, shop.
- **Floating at a planet.** No jobs (the board is empty, Accept says why), no refuel, no ships, no upgrades, no outposts: each gives a funny plain-text reason with the planet's name. Switching ship and the emergency refuel work. Leaving counts the distance from that planet's star system.
- **Strand protection.** A trip is refused if, after it, you could not reach a dock with the fuel left. Every star system has at least one dock, so the reserve is the fuel for a hop inside the target system, at least 1 unit (engine upgrades never go below 1). The text has the numbers: "Not enough fuel to come back safely: after this trip you would have 0 fuel and need 1 to reach a dock. Refuel first, or pick a closer place." (Floating: "Pick a closer place.") Plus the old texts "Not enough fuel: needs N, you have F" and "Too far for this ship's tank".
- **Emergency refuel while floating.** Allowed when your fuel is less than the hop to a dock (only possible after switching to a ship with an empty tank, or in a test). Gives 20 fuel for free, the same funny lines. The dock version is unchanged.
- **Discovery credits.** The first arrival in a star system (free travel or a job) pays 50 to 100 credits, chosen by seed + system id (same every time), once per system, ever. Settings: `DISCOVERY_MIN_CREDITS`, `DISCOVERY_MAX_CREDITS`. Job arrivals report it on the `Arrival` (`discovery`, `newSystem`, `newPlanet`, `explorerRankUp`); the job's `reward` is unchanged. Your start system is NOT counted at the start; it counts (and pays) when you arrive there later.
- **Explorer rank (Andy chose option B).** Discoveries = star systems explored + planets (docks and scenery) arrived at. Rank 2 "Cartographer" at 50, rank 3 "Mr Galaxy-wide" at 200; rank 1 is "Lost in Space". Rank raises only discovery credits: +0% / +10% / +20% (rank before the arrival counts). `game.getExplorer()` gives systems, planets, discoveries, rank, next rank, discoveries to go. It is separate from `getRanks()` (the three job ranks are untouched, so no screen changes before F2).
- **Save.** `explored` is stored compactly: star system id -> one number whose bits are the planets arrived at (a system has at most 10 planets, so the number is below 1024). About 24 bytes per system: 1,000 systems = about 24 KB; the cap `MAX_EXPLORED_SYSTEMS = 20000` (over 1,000 game hours of exploring) is under 1 MB; at the cap new systems are simply not recorded. `SAVE_VERSION` stays 1 and `lazy-freight.*` keys are unchanged (new fields have defaults, as in 4d). Universe v2 migration clears `explored`, a float in space and a free trip (fuel of a cancelled free trip is given back). `fitToUniverse` and `Game.loadState` send you back to your dock if a saved place does not exist or is a dock.
- **`/game-debug.html`:** status shows where you are (docked / floating) and the Explorer line; new section "10. Free travel and exploring" lists every place in this star system and the 6 nearest others, with the fuel numbers or the refusal reason and a "Fly here" button; TEST buttons: fuel to 3, fuel to exactly the strand amount, add 24 made-up explored systems (the next arrival ranks up), forget explored, show the explored list as saved. The fast-forward button and the last-trip lines handle free trips.
- **Simulator:** `tests/sim/explorer-sim.ts` plays a no-job explorer with the real rules; `npm run balance` has a new "Explorer" table.

## 5. Data for the Lead (no prices retuned, no job numbers changed)
- **Explorer simulator (12 game hours, 8 seeds, medians):** docks-only explorer: 105 systems, 144 planets, rank 2 at 2.4 h, rank 3 at 9.7 h; visits-every-planet explorer: 88 systems, 482 planets, rank 2 at 1.1 h, rank 3 at 4.3 h. Never stranded, never stuck, strand refusals 0 (the explorer refuels at docks). Credits: +8,462 discovery against 1,428 fuel bought: exploring pays for itself (about +45 credits per system net).
- **Job players explore by accident** (jobs count): sensible reaches about 31 systems and 32 planets in 10 h (Cartographer at about 8 h) and about 190 discoveries in 40 h; casual about 47 in 40 h (Cartographer near 40 h).
- **Effect on the job economy:** discovery credits (+50 to 100 per new system) are now part of every job player's income: about +5% for the sensible player (about 3 new systems an hour). First-outpost times in `npm run balance` (40 h): planner 10.1 h (4e: 11.1), sensible 32.8 h (29.0), spender 32.1 h (27.1), casual and lazy never. These moved by a few hours because every run's buying order depends on earlier credits; the economy is not worse, only different. Not retuned.
