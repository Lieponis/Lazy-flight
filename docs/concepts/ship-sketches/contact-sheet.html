Ship idea sketches

Inspiration only, not part of the game. Rough 2D concept sketches of 9 possible future ships (side view on top, top view below), in the same low-poly style and colours as today's ships. Nothing here is decided; the game still has its three ships. No weapons on any ship.

Three groups, each with a mid-range, a long-haul and a heavy super-long-haul ship. In each group's size line-up the first ship is one of today's ships, drawn at the same scale.

Cargo

From a tidy container hauler to a giant tanker.

Size line-up (same scale, side view)

Crate Expectations

Mid range · size: Medium · about 9 crates long · file: cargo-1.svg

Role: Wedge-nosed hauler with four containers clamped in a cradle under its belly

“Never late, never early, always slightly too full.”

Box Office

Long haul · size: Large · about 16 crates long · file: cargo-2.svg

Role: Modular freighter: an angular cockpit, a long frame of clamped container modules, side thrusters and a three-nozzle engine

“Knows every loading dock in the sector by its first name.”

Big Thirsty

Heavy super long haul · size: Huge · about 29 crates long · file: cargo-3.svg

Role: Giant angular tanker hull (Type-9 style) with four big tanks set into its sides, a small cockpit high on the nose and four angled engine nacelles

“Slow, calm and enormous; small ships politely wait for it to pass.”

Passenger

A space bus, a long-haul yacht and a cruise liner.

Size line-up (same scale, side view)

Sunday Shuttle

Mid range · size: Medium · about 7 crates long · file: passenger-1.svg

Role: Little angular shuttle with one long window strip, canted fins and two engines

“Stops everywhere, waves at everyone, smells faintly of toast.”

Velvet Comet

Long haul · size: Large · about 15 crates long · file: passenger-2.svg

Role: Sleek long-haul yacht (Dolphin/Orca style): a long swept hull, one big glazed lounge strip, swept wings with wingtip engines and three main thrusters

“Serves tiny sandwiches at warp speed and never spills a drop.”

Grand Snoozer

Heavy super long haul · size: Huge · about 29 crates long · file: passenger-3.svg

Role: Huge cruise liner (Beluga style): a big rounded-angular head with a panoramic glass deck, glazed promenade bands, a garden dome and four engine pods on pylons

“So comfy that passengers forget where they were going, and do not mind.”

Explorer

Angular, wide, full of dishes, tanks and pods: built to reach the rim of the galaxy.

Size line-up (same scale, side view)

Pebble Picker

Mid range · size: Medium · about 9 crates long · file: explorer-1.svg

Role: Angular wide-body survey ship with a sample arm, side sensor pods and a small dish

“Collects interesting rocks and gives each one a name.”

Long Way Round

Long haul · size: Large · about 19 crates long · file: explorer-2.svg

Role: Deep-range explorer: huge fuel tanks, a big dish and a tiny research dome

“Has seen the edge of the galaxy and still stops to look at every rock.”

Horizon Ark

Heavy super long haul · size: Huge · about 29 crates long · file: explorer-3.svg

Role: Mothership for the galaxy rim: a wide angular hull, solar wings, three dishes, greenhouse domes and a tiny probe in its hangar

“A flying research town that sends postcards home from places with no names.”
