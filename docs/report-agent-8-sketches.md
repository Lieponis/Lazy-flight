# Report: Agent 8, ship idea sketches (inspiration only), 2026-11-18

## What was drawn

Rough 2D concept sketches of 9 possible future ships, each with a side view, a top view and a scale bar. Every ship
has a name, a range class, a size, a role and a one-line personality. None of them has weapons.

| Group | Mid range | Long haul | Heavy super long haul |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cargo | Crate Expectations (about 9 crates long) | Box Office (16) | Big Thirsty (29) |
| Passenger | Sunday Shuttle (7) | Velvet Comet (15) | Grand Snoozer (29) |
| Explorer | Pebble Picker (9) | Long Way Round (19) | Horizon Ark (29) |

How Andy shaped them:
- **Inspiration:** "just surprise me", taking ideas from Elite Dangerous, No Man's Sky and Starfield, with a variety of
  sizes and shapes.
- **Style:** A, flat game colours with dark outlines, chosen over a blueprint style.
- **Three range classes per group:**
  - mid range is about the size of today's Passenger Liner;
  - long haul is bigger, with more windows, containers and engines;
  - heavy super long haul is cruise-ship or tanker size.
- **Passenger proportions:** bus, yacht and cruise ship.
- **Explorers:** angular, with wider bodies and extra attachments.
- **Revision 2:** the cargo and passenger ships first looked too much like sea ships. They were redrawn as spaceships
  in the Elite Dangerous and Starfield manner: angular wedge hulls, modular frames, glazed strips, landing legs and
  angled engines.

## Where the files are

All files are in `docs/concepts/ship-sketches/`:
- `contact-sheet.html`: all 9 ships, with a size line-up per group that starts with one of today's ships. Readable on
  a phone, no scripts.
- `cargo-1.svg` to `explorer-3.svg`: one drawing per ship.
- `README.md`: what is where, what it means. These are inspiration only, not part of the game.

Nothing else changed: no game code, models, catalog, tests or workflows. The game's three-ship rule is untouched.

## What Andy liked

- **Explorers:** he really liked them, with no changes.
- **Cargo and passenger:** OK after revision 2.
- **"All are okay":** he will use the sketches as inspiration to design new ships himself.

Nothing was asked for now. Any new ship (a 3D model in the game) would be a separate decision for Andy and the Lead.

**Suggestion:** if a new ship is wanted later, the explorers are the natural first candidates, since Andy liked them most.
